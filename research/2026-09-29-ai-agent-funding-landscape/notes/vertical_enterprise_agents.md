# Funding & Traction of Enterprise / Vertical AI Agent Companies (excl. coding agents), Oct 2025 – Sept 2026

Note on source quality: primary sources (company blogs, press releases), TechCrunch, Bloomberg, CNBC, Forbes and Sifted are marked as high confidence. Aggregators (Sacra, GetLatka, valueaddvc.com, aifundingtracker, arr.club, tech-insider, lawyer-monthly) are marked "[aggregator]" and should be treated as reported and unverified. Research was capped at about 18 tool calls, so coverage of long-tail companies is incomplete (see Gaps).

## Customer support / CX agents (incl. voice)

### Takeaway
Customer support and CX is the most heavily capitalized agent function outside coding. In roughly 9 months (Jan to May 2026) it absorbed about $2.0B+ across just four headline rounds: Sierra $950M, ElevenLabs $500M, Parloa $350M and Decagon $250M. It also has some of the best revenue evidence: Sierra says it reached $150M ARR by Feb 2026, and Intercom Fin is near $100M ARR on per-resolution pricing.

### Cited Findings
**Sierra** (autonomous CX agents for enterprises, founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor)
- Raised $950M on 2026-05-04, co-led by Tiger Global and GV, at a post-money valuation "above $15 billion". — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/)
- Aggregators put the valuation at $15.8B, with Benchmark, Sequoia and Greenoaks participating and about $1.6B raised in total. — [GetLatka [aggregator]](https://getlatka.com/companies/sierra); [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/sierra-raises-950m-at-15b-valuation-eyes-transformation-beyond-customer-support/)
- Traction: $100M ARR in late Nov 2025 (about 7 quarters after its Feb 2024 launch) and $150M ARR by early Feb 2026. More than 40% of the Fortune 50 are customers. Its agents handle mortgage refinancing, insurance claims, returns and nonprofit fundraising. Ghostwriter, an agent-builder, launched in Apr 2026. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/)
- ~$200M ARR in 2026 (reported, unverified). — [GetLatka [aggregator]](https://getlatka.com/companies/sierra)
- Sierra is widely described as using outcome-based pricing. — [valueaddvc [aggregator]](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/how-does-sierra-ai-make-money-outcome-based-pricing-enterprise-agents-and-the-business-model-breakdown)

**Decagon** (AI "concierge" customer support agents)
- Series D of $250M, reported 2026-01-28. Valuation of $4.5B, triple the $1.5B Series C. Co-led by Coatue and Index. Chemistry, Definition and Starwood participated, along with existing backers a16z, Accel and Bain Capital Ventures. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-28/ai-customer-support-startup-decagon-valued-at-4-5-billion); [Decagon blog](https://decagon.ai/blog/series-d-announcement); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/decagon-raises-250-million-valuation-161223745.html)
- Traction: more than 100 new enterprise customers in the fiscal year, including Avis Budget, Block and Deutsche Telekom. An employee tender offer at $4.5B followed in Mar 2026. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/decagon-raises-250-million-valuation-161223745.html)
- Background: $131M Series C at a $1.5B valuation (mid-2025). — [Decagon blog](https://decagon.ai/blog/series-c-announcement)

**Parloa** (Berlin; enterprise voice and CX agents)
- $350M Series D on 2026-01-15 at a $3B valuation, led by General Catalyst. EQT Ventures, Altimeter, Durable and Mosaic participated. This tripled its valuation from the $1B set about 7–8 months earlier, when it raised $120M. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/15/parloa-triples-its-valuation-in-8-months-to-3b-with-350m-raise/); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/parloa-triples-valuation-350m-series-d-general-catalyst); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parloa-valued-at-3-billion-with-350m-series-d-to-lead-agentic-ai-for-customer-experience-302662228.html)

**ElevenLabs** (voice AI; its Conversational/Agents platform is one product line)
- $500M Series D on 2026-02-04 at an $11B valuation, more than triple its valuation a year earlier. Sequoia led (Andrew Reed joined the board), and a16z and ICONIQ invested super pro-rata. Total raised is $781M. — [ElevenLabs blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/04/elevenlabs-raises-500m-from-sequioia-at-a-11-billion-valuation/); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-04/ai-startup-elevenlabs-more-than-triples-valuation-to-11-billion)
- ~$500M ARR (reported, unverified; not broken out for agents). — [GetLatka [aggregator]](https://getlatka.com/companies/elevenlabs.io)

**Intercom Fin** (traction and pricing signal)
- Fin is nearing $100M ARR, roughly half of Intercom's total revenue. It is priced at $0.99 per resolved conversation. The CFO says the resolution rate rose from about 25% at launch to 65–70%. Reported Aug 2026. — [Enterprise DNA [aggregator]](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/ai-pulse/ai-pulse-2026-08-02-intercom-s-fin-ai-agent-is-nearing-100m-arr-roughly-half-of/); pricing confirmed by [Fin help center](https://fin.ai/help/en/articles/13975800-fin-pricing-outcomes)

**Voice-agent infrastructure (Retell, Vapi, Bland, Synthflow)**
- Market commentary splits the space into lanes. Vapi and ElevenLabs are developer infrastructure. Retell, Bland and Synthflow are managed platforms. Parloa is the enterprise CX option. — [StackBriefly](https://stackbriefly.com/blog/ai-voice-agents-2026-vapi-retell-elevenlabs-bland-synthflow)
- No Oct 2025–Sept 2026 funding figures were found for Retell, Vapi, Bland, Synthflow, Crescendo or Giga (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- CX valuations roughly tripled in 6–8 months for Decagon, Parloa and ElevenLabs. The category is priced as a winner-take-most market.
- Sierra is the scale leader. At about $15B on $150M ARR, it trades near 100x run-rate.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 rounds were found for Crescendo, Giga, Retell, Vapi, Bland or Synthflow, and Decagon's ARR was not found in a primary source.

## Sales / GTM / marketing agents

### Takeaway
Clay is the category's clearest revenue story, with $150M ARR in May 2026. Rox is the notable new unicorn. The AI-SDR sub-segment (11x, Artisan) carries the category's main credibility problem, stemming from the 2025 11x allegations.

### Cited Findings
- **Clay** (GTM data and enrichment, now "sales agents"). $115M Series D at a $7.1B post-money valuation, reported Sept 2026. Wellington is reported as a participant, at a $7B pre-money. ARR grew from $108M at end-2025 to $150M in May 2026, after 4x revenue growth in 2025. — [Runtimewire](https://runtimewire.com/article/kareem-amin-clay-115m-series-d-7-1b-valuation); [valueaddvc [aggregator]](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/clay-115m-series-d-7-1b-valuation-2026); [Sacra [aggregator]](https://sacra.com/c/clay/)
- **Rox** (AI agents for revenue teams). Reportedly raised at a $1.2B valuation led by General Catalyst, with about $8M ARR projected by end-2025. The exact date and amount were not confirmed. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/roxai)
- **Artisan** ("Ava" AI BDR). $25M Series A in Apr 2025 (older background). About $48M raised in total. — [TechCrunch 2025-04-09](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/09/artisan-the-stop-hiring-humans-ai-agent-startup-raises-25m-and-is-still-hiring-humans/)
- **11x** (AI SDR "digital workers"). In Mar 2025 (older background), TechCrunch reported the following:
  - 11x listed ZoomInfo and Airtable as customers. Both denied it, and ZoomInfo threatened legal action.
  - ARR was inflated by counting short trials as annual contracts: about $10M claimed versus about $3M retained.
  - Churn was reportedly 70–80%.
  - The CEO disputed the claims. a16z and Benchmark publicly backed the team.
  - — [Summary via Salesmotion](https://salesmotion.io/blog/turns-out-ai-sdrs-are-too-good-to-be-true-11x-might-face-legal-action); [OnlyCFO](https://www.onlycfo.io/p/ai-company-accused-of-fraud)

### Inferences
- Money flowing to Clay and Rox rather than AI-SDR "replacement" plays suggests investors favor data and workflow platforms with agents layered on top over pure autonomous-SDR bets after the 11x episode.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 rounds or ARR were found for 11x, Artisan, Unify or Qualified, and the date of Rox's round is unconfirmed.

## Legal

### Takeaway
Legal is the second-hottest vertical after CX, and the Harvey vs. Legora race sets its valuations:
- **Harvey:** $11B valuation in Mar 2026, with reported talks at about $15B.
- **Legora:** $5.6B valuation in Apr 2026, reportedly seeking $10B+.

Both show verified ARR above $100M.

### Cited Findings
- **Harvey.**
  - $200M on 2026-03-25 at an $11B valuation, co-led by GIC and Sequoia. a16z, Coatue, Conviction, Elad Gil, Evantic and Kleiner Perkins participated. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/25/legal-ai-startup-harvey-raises-200-million-at-11-billion-valuation.html); [Harvey blog](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/harvey-raises-growth-round-at-dollar11-billion-valuation-co-led-by-gic-and-sequoia)
  - Traction: more than 100,000 lawyers across 1,300 organizations, including most of the AmLaw 100. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/legal-ai-startup-legora-hits-5-6-valuation-and-its-battle-with-harvey-just-got-hotter/)
  - ~$190M ARR by Jan 2026, up from $100M in Aug 2025 (reported). — [valueaddvc [aggregator]](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/harvey-ai-valuation-revenue-2026-legal-ai-11b)
  - Reported to be in talks at about $15B in Aug 2026. — [Lawyer Monthly](https://www.lawyer-monthly.com/2026/08/legora-10bn-valuation-harvey-15bn-ai-funding/)
- **Legora** (Sweden).
  - $550M Series D in Mar 2026, plus a $50M extension in Apr 2026, for $600M in total at a $5.6B post-money valuation. NVentures and Atlassian invested.
  - At that time it reported $100M+ ARR and 1,000+ customers in 50 markets, including Linklaters, Cleary Gottlieb and Bird & Bird.
  - — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/legal-ai-startup-legora-hits-5-6-valuation-and-its-battle-with-harvey-just-got-hotter/)
  - Reported $200M ARR in Sept 2026, doubling in under 6 months, and seeking a $10B+ valuation (reported, not closed). — [Lawyer Monthly](https://www.lawyer-monthly.com/2026/08/legora-10bn-valuation-harvey-15bn-ai-funding/)
- **EvenUp** (personal-injury demand letters and claims). $150M Series E on 2025-10-07 at about a $2B valuation. About $385M raised in total, from backers including Bessemer, BCV and SignalFire. — [Tracxn [aggregator]](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/evenup/__jWAN42CkOwP4Ke_seuKhPhF4XouwhlzyOgOyrH1np0E)
- **Eudia** (in-house legal agents). $105M Series A led by General Catalyst in Feb 2025 (older background). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eudia_(company))

### Inferences
- Legora's reported ARR doubling while trailing Harvey on valuation suggests legal valuations are converging toward revenue multiples of about 50x.

### Gaps
- Harvey's ARR has no primary-source confirmation for 2026. No 2026 rounds were found for EvenUp or Eudia.

## Healthcare

### Takeaway
Healthcare AI draws some of the largest valuations, led by OpenEvidence ($12B in Jan 2026, reportedly $15B in Sept 2026). However, OpenEvidence is a clinician-search product rather than a workflow agent. Ambient scribes (Abridge, Ambience) and patient-facing voice agents (Hippocratic) are the true "agent" plays.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenEvidence** (clinical evidence assistant for doctors).
  - $250M Series D in Jan 2026 at $12B, led by Thrive and DST, doubling from $6B about 3 months earlier (Oct 2025).
  - A further $250M at $15B, led by Byers and a16z, in Sept 2026 (reported).
  - Reportedly used by about 40% of US doctors.
  - — [valueaddvc [aggregator]](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/openevidence-valuation-2026-12b-round-40-percent-of-us-doctors-and-why-it-moved-to-miami); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/openevidence/)
- **Abridge** (ambient clinical documentation). $300M Series E at $5.3B in Jun 2025 (background), plus a reported $316M extension in Apr 2026. Reported $100M+ ARR. Ranked #30 on the CNBC Disruptor 50 (May 2026). — [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/ambient-ai-startup-abridge-scores-300m-series-e-backed-a16z-and-khosla); [aifundingtracker [aggregator]](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-ai-healthcare-startups/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/abridge-cnbc-disruptor-50-ranking.html)
- **Ambience Healthcare**. $243M at $1.25B in Jul 2025 (background). — [aifundingtracker [aggregator]](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-ai-healthcare-startups/)
- **Hippocratic AI** (patient-facing healthcare voice agents). $126M Series C at a $3.5B valuation, bringing total funding to $404M. The date was not confirmed in the fetched sources; it is believed to be late 2025. — [Hippocratic AI](https://hippocraticai.com/hippocratic-ai-announces-series-c-funding-126-million/)
- **Tennr** (referral and patient intake automation). Listed among US AI startups that raised $100M+ in 2025 ($101M). — [TechCrunch 2026-01-19](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/19/here-are-the-49-us-ai-startups-that-have-raised-100m-or-more-in-2025/)

### Gaps
- There is no primary confirmation of OpenEvidence's Sept 2026 $15B round or Abridge's Apr 2026 extension, and no 2026 figures for Ambience or Tennr.

## Finance / accounting

### Cited Findings

- **Rillet** (AI-native ERP and general ledger). $100M Series C at a $1B valuation led by ICONIQ, reported Aug 2026, bringing total raised above $200M. It reports 600+ customers and says new ARR doubled in the prior quarter. — [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/rillet-becomes-unicorn-100m-series-c-iconiq/); [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/accounting-startup-rillet-hits-1-billion-valuation-as-ai-eats-cfo-busywork/)
- **Campfire** (AI ERP). $35M Series A led by Accel in Jun 2025, then a $65M Series B co-led by Accel and Ribbit months later (around late 2025). — [Landbase summary](https://www.landbase.com/blog/fastest-growing-accounting-tech-companies-startups)

### Gaps

### Cited Findings

- **By capital:** customer support/CX (plus voice) raised the most, followed by legal and healthcare.
- **By revenue evidence:** the strongest is in CX (Sierra; Intercom Fin), legal (Harvey, Legora) and GTM data (Clay).
- **By pricing:** outcome-based pricing (per resolution) is the defining shift in CX.
- **Failures:** the main ARR-quality warning is 11x (2025). Mercor's gross-versus-net revenue is a live 2026 quality debate.

- Outcome-based pricing appears to work where the output is a discrete, measurable event, such as a resolved ticket. Seat pricing persists in legal and finance.

### Gaps
- No down-rounds, shutdowns or pivots in this category during Oct 2025–Sept 2026 were found. That is a search limitation, not evidence that none occurred.
