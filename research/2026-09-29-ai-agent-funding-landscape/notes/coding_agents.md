# AI Coding / Software-Engineering Agents and Vibe-Coding App Builders: Funding and Traction (Oct 2025 – Sept 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Confidence convention: **[Primary]** = company announcement or tier-1 outlet (TechCrunch, CNBC, Bloomberg, Axios, BusinessWire/PRNewswire); **[Secondary]** = aggregator/analyst blog (Sacra, valueaddvc, tech-insider, getlatka, gradually.ai, etc.), treat as unverified; **[Rumored]** = "in talks" reporting.

## Q1: Major rounds, Oct 2025 – Sept 2026 (amount, valuation, leads, date)

### Takeaway
Money in this category went mostly to a few leaders, and their valuations rose 2-3x within months. Cursor went from $29.3B (Nov 2025) to a $60B all-stock sale to SpaceX (closed Aug 14, 2026). Cognition went from $10.2B to $26B (May 2026). Lovable went from $6.6B to $13.3B (Dec 2025 to Aug 2026). Replit went from $3B to $9B (Sept 2025 to Mar 2026). Emergent went from $300M to $1.5B (Jan to Jul 2026). Factory is at $1.5B (Apr 2026). The largest event of the period was an acquisition, not a funding round.

### Inferences
- Valuations roughly doubled or tripled every 6–8 months at every leader (Cursor, Cognition, Lovable, Replit, Emergent). Investors paid about 25–50x forward run-rate: Lovable $13.3B on about $500–600M, Replit $9B on about $525M (secondary figure), Cognition $26B on $492M.
- The biggest outcome was strategic M&A (SpaceX–Cursor), not an IPO. Cursor also acted as a consolidator itself (Graphite). This suggests the leaders are buying adjacent segments such as code review.
- The investor base is mostly the same repeat names: a16z, Thrive, Accel, Coatue, Menlo, CapitalG/Google, NVIDIA, Khosla, Founders Fund.

### Gaps
- I could not verify Poolside's 2026 round, Magic, Augment Code (secondary sources say only "raised >$200M" total), Sourcegraph/Amp, Warp, Rocket, Bolt, CodeRabbit or Greptile follow-on rounds within the window. Searches returned only product descriptions or pre-window data.

Lovable went from $200M to $500M (Nov 2025 to Jun 2026). The labs are growing as fast or faster: Claude Code passed a $2.5B run-rate by Feb 2026, and Codex had more than 5M weekly users by June 2026.

- Customer segments are splitting: enterprise/autonomous products (Cognition, Factory) on one side, and consumer/prosumer app builders (Lovable, Replit, Emergent) with very large user counts but low ARPU on the other.

### Gaps

## Q3: Which sub-segments are hot

### Takeaway
The largest rounds went to IDE agents (Cursor, and Windsurf inside Cognition), autonomous background agents (Cognition/Devin, Factory) and non-developer app builders (Lovable, Replit, Emergent). Code review is consolidating through M&A (Graphite into Cursor). I found little dated 2026 funding evidence for testing/QA, migration or security within this search.

### Cited Findings
- IDE agent: Cursor, with a $60B exit and about $4B ARR. [Primary] — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-spcx-cursor-acquisition-ipo.html)
- Autonomous background agents: Cognition at $26B; Factory at $1.5B for enterprise, model-agnostic Droids. [Primary] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1b-at-25b-pre-money-valuation/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/factory-hits-1-5b-valuation-to-build-ai-coding-for-enterprises/)
- App builders for non-developers: Lovable at $13.3B, Replit at $9B, Emergent at $1.5B. These are the fastest valuation multipliers. [Primary] — see Q1 citations.
- Code review: Graphite was acquired by Cursor (Dec 2025). CodeRabbit ($550M, Sept 2025) and Greptile ($25M A) remain independent. Greptile exposes review feedback to coding agents via MCP. [Primary/Secondary] — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/23/greptile-bags-25m-funding-take-coderabbit-graphite-ai-code-validation/); [Graphite blog](https://graphite.com/blog/graphite-joins-cursor)
- Terminal/CLI agents: Warp says its agent ships more than 50% of its own PRs. Sourcegraph's Amp moved to a free, ad-supported tier. [Secondary] — [datalakehousehub](https://datalakehousehub.com/blog/agentic-coding-tools/)

### Inferences
- Code review and verification of agent output is becoming a strategic chokepoint, which explains Cursor's purchase of Graphite. Expect more M&A in review/testing as agent-written code volume rises (Cognition says 89% of its own commits come from Devin).

### Gaps
- I found no dated Oct 2025–Sept 2026 rounds for testing/QA (e.g., QA Wolf, Momentic), legacy migration/modernization, or code security startups in this pass. These need a dedicated search.

## Q4: Down-rounds, acqui-hires, shutdowns, margin worries

### Takeaway
There were no notable down-rounds among the leaders in the window; valuations only went up. The main negatives are structural: gross margins that are negative or thin because of inference costs, which was flagged in Aug 2025 and has not been disclosed since, and acqui-hire style exits.

### Cited Findings
- Aug 2025 (background): Windsurf's gross margins were "very negative." Code-gen margins were described as "either neutral or negative" across Cursor, Lovable, Replit and others, and Anysphere was said to run negative gross margins because of power users on flat-rate plans. [Primary] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/07/the-high-costs-and-thin-margins-threatening-ai-coding-startups/)
- Jason Lemkin argued that many vibe-coding platforms pay model providers more than they charge for the same tokens, but that prosumer apps (Replit, Lovable) may be fine. [Opinion] — [X/@jasonlk](https://x.com/jasonlk/status/1954561859236896777); [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/inference-is-the-new-sales-marketing-spend)
- None of Lovable, Bolt, Replit or v0 have disclosed gross margins. Credit-based pricing can mask worsening unit economics. [Secondary] — [valueaddvc](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/lovable-valuation-2026-13-2b-and-500m-arr-how-vibe-coding-actually-makes-money)
- Mitigations: Lovable now offers an in-house trained model and signed a Google Cloud compute deal (June 2026). Factory routes across models, including cheaper DeepSeek. [Primary] — [TechCrunch Lovable](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/); [TechCrunch Factory](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/factory-hits-1-5b-valuation-to-build-ai-coding-for-enterprises/)
- Acqui-hires (background, 2025): Google's Windsurf leadership acqui-hire and licensing deal, after which Cognition bought the remainder. Cursor acqui-hired Koala's team in July 2025, and Koala then shut down. [Primary/Secondary] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1b-at-25b-pre-money-valuation/); [Wikipedia: Cursor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cursor_(company))
- In the broader AI market, one tracker's list of shutdowns, acqui-hires and quiet pivots grew from 14 (start of 2025) to 47 (Mar 2026). Big Tech spent more than $20B on "reverse acqui-hires" between Mar 2024 and Jan 2026. [Secondary, not coding-specific] — [Medium](https://medium.com/@svnkrmkr/the-reverse-acquihire-the-ai-startup-exit-nobody-explains-cc2d48ea81e7)

### Inferences
- The margin problem has not been disproven, only left undisclosed. Strategic buyers or backers with compute (SpaceX/xAI for Cursor, Google Cloud for Lovable, NVIDIA/Google in Cursor's Series D) are one answer to it: owning or subsidizing inference.
- Second-tier coding tools without a distinct wedge face pressure from Claude Code, Codex and Copilot bundling. Acqui-hire or tuck-in exits into leaders (the Graphite pattern) look more likely than down-rounds.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed coding-specific down-rounds or shutdowns in Oct 2025–Sept 2026. The status of Magic.dev, Poolside's commercial traction and Bolt's 2026 trajectory is unknown.
- I found no 2026 disclosure of gross margins by any independent coding company.
