# VC Theses and Aggregate Funding Data for AI Agents (as of 2026-09-29)

Methodology caveat that runs through every section: no major data provider publishes a clean, public "AI agent" funding series. Crunchbase and CB Insights publish AI-wide totals (which are dominated by OpenAI/Anthropic/xAI mega-rounds). PitchBook has an "agentic AI" analyst-note series, but it is paywalled (403 on fetch), so its figures here come from search snippets. Agent-specific trackers (gravity.fast, callsphere, newmarketpitch) are small blogs with narrow or unclear definitions, and their numbers disagree with each other by an order of magnitude. Treat agent-only numbers as low confidence and AI-wide numbers as high confidence.

## 1. What aggregate datasets report (totals, quarters, deals, mega-rounds, valuations)

### Takeaway
AI-wide venture funding hit records in H1 2026: Crunchbase counts about $510B globally, with more than 70% going to AI. The totals are driven by a handful of lab mega-rounds (OpenAI plus Anthropic took 43%), and deal counts are flat. Agent-specific funding is much smaller and poorly measured. PitchBook's figure of $24.2B across 1,311 agentic-AI deals in 2025 is the best anchor. Estimates for 2026 run from about $1B (narrow definitions) to $12B+ per quarter (broad definitions).

### Cited Findings
**AI-wide / all-VC totals (high confidence)**

That compares with $440B for all of 2025 and a prior half-year record of $375B in H2 2021. Anthropic raised $65B in Q2 alone. — [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-exits-ipo-ma-soar-ai-q2-h1-2026/)

Not verified in a Carta primary document.

### Inferences
- The AI-wide headline numbers (70–86% of VC) overstate "agent" funding by an order of magnitude, because roughly 40–45% of all venture dollars went to two labs. Agent application and infrastructure companies probably raised in the low tens of billions per year: PitchBook's 2025 figure is $24.2B, and 2026 is likely higher given Cognition, Factory, Sierra-type rounds and coding-agent valuations.
- The pattern is concentration without breadth. Mega-rounds make up 53–89% of dollars (depending on dataset) while deal counts stay flat. In agents, the top 10 deals take about 78% of capital. The market is bifurcating into a few category leaders and a long tail with median rounds around $30M.
- Methodology differences between the datasets:
  - Crunchbase and CB Insights both count global equity rounds and revise upward as data comes in.
  - PitchBook-NVCA is US-only.
  - CB Insights "AI" includes all AI-tagged companies.
  - Carta reflects only companies on its cap-table platform, which skews early-stage and US.
  - The agent trackers use editorial definitions.

### Gaps
- Dealroom and KPMG Venture Pulse Q2 2026 agent-specific figures were not retrieved.
- PitchBook's full agentic AI note and its Q1/Q2 2026 AI VC Trends reports (both 403 on fetch) could not be read. There are no quarterly agent series for Q1/Q2 2026 from a primary data provider.
- No Q3 2026 aggregate (quarter ends 2026-09-30) is available yet from Crunchbase, PitchBook or CB Insights.
- Agent-specific seed and Series A valuation medians were not found. Only AI-wide premiums are available.

## 2. Published VC theses: what categories firms favor or avoid, and why

### Takeaway
The consensus 2026 frame is "services-as-software." Firms want to sell outcomes and capture labor budgets rather than software budgets: Sequoia's "autopilots," General Catalyst's AI roll-ups, and Foundation Capital. There are also strong bets on coding agents, vertical agents with measurable ROI, and agent infrastructure (governance, security, observability, evals, payments). Investors say they avoid thin wrappers, horizontal copilots that labs will absorb, and "supernova" revenue with poor retention or margins.

### Cited Findings
- **Sequoia**: Julien Bek's "Services: The New Software" (2026-03-05) argues the next $1T company will be "a software company masquerading as a services firm." For every $1 spent on software, $6 is spent on services. "A copilot sells the tool, an autopilot sells the work." Favored targets are outsourced, intelligence-heavy work with a dash of judgment: insurance brokerage, claims adjustment, IT managed services, tax advisory, accounting and audit, simple legal, payroll and compliance. — [Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software); [Fortune, 2026-04-21](https://fortune.com/2026/04/21/services-are-the-new-software-sequoia-venture-capital-julien-bek-ai-native-eye-on-ai/)
- "Services as software" is described as the default B2B frame, with Foundation Capital using the same language. — [Peter Tippett Substack](https://petertippett.substack.com/p/what-the-ai-native-services-playbook) (via search summary; commentary)
- **a16z**: Anish Acharya's "Notes on AI Apps in 2026" (2026-01-08) favors specialized apps over labs, because labs are "jagged" and constrained by competing priorities. He argues coding tools produced more than $1B of new revenue in 2025 and that "every team should be a software team," pointing to domain products (Harvey) and "bare metal agents" (Codex, Claude Code). He sees the next frontier in "thinking" tools, because "as coding agents work with increasing accuracy, the hard problem moves from how do I build it to what do I build." He is wary that models are poor at deciding what to build. — [a16z](https://a16z.com/notes-on-ai-apps-in-2026/)
- a16z Big Ideas 2026 argues the system of record starts to lose primacy as agentic workflows execute end-to-end processes. — [a16z Big Ideas 2026](https://a16z.com/newsletter/big-ideas-2026-part-1/) (via search summary)
- **General Catalyst**: it has committed $1.5B since late 2023 to its "Creation" AI-enabled roll-up strategy. The firm reviewed about 70 service areas and picked about 10 where current AI can automate 30–70% of tasks. Portfolio includes Titan (IT MSP; $74M raise plus acquisition of RFA), Eudia (acquired legal consultancy Johnson Hana, July 2025), Long Lake, Dwelly and Crescendo. PitchBook covered a $6.3B "AMEX deal" as a display of this strategy. — [Capital and Clarity](https://capitalandclarity.substack.com/p/the-general-catalyst-behind-15-billion); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalysts-6-3b-amex-deal-puts-its-ai-roll-up-strategy-on-display); [General Catalyst, "The Future of Services"](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/the-future-of-services)
- **PitchBook** commentary: "AI agents are winning by selling workflows," pointing to outcome-based business models. — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/ai-agents-software-business-model-outcomes-based) (title only; body not retrieved)
- **Menlo Ventures** (2025 State of GenAI in the Enterprise, published 2025-12-09):
  - Enterprise GenAI spend reached $37B in 2025, 3.2x YoY and about 6% of global SaaS.
  - 76% of use cases are purchased rather than built.
  - Only 16% of enterprise deployments qualify as "true agents"; most are fixed-sequence workflows.
  - Enterprise LLM API share: Anthropic 40%, OpenAI 27%, Google 21%.
  - Menlo also co-runs the $100M Anthology Fund with Anthropic.
  - — [Menlo Ventures](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/09/3202258/0/en/Menlo-Ventures-2025-State-of-Generative-AI-Report-Enterprise-Investment-Hit-37B-in-2025-Tripling-in-One-Year.html)
- **Bessemer** (State of AI 2025) defines two archetypes. "Supernovas" reach about $100M ARR in about 1.5 years "often with fragile retention and thin margins." "Shooting Stars" grow from $3M to $100M over about 4 years with healthy margins. Bessemer expects evals to go "private, grounded, and trusted" and the browser to become the key agent interface. — [Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-state-of-ai-2025)
- **CB Insights 2026 agent predictions** (2026-02-26):
  - Multimodal agents win customer service.
  - Voice AI moves to on-site "embedded engineer" deployment models.
  - Continuous red-teaming becomes standard.
  - Agent observability and evals become an M&A battleground.
  - World models power physical agents.
  - — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/ai-agent-predictions-2026/)
- CB Insights Q1'26 warns that for startups outside the frontier tier, "the window for differentiation is narrowing." — [CB Insights Q1'26](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/ai-trends-q1-2026/) (via search summary)
- The Q3 2026 tracker finds infrastructure for agents (payments, training stacks, search), the governance and control layer, and vertical agents with customers getting funded, and thin wrappers not. — [gravity.fast](https://gravity.fast/blog/ai-agent-funding-tracker-q3-2026/)
- Coding agents are the largest-valued category: Cognition at $48B (Sept 2026, a16z and Accel) and Factory at $5B (Sept 2026). Crunchbase reports a "SpaceX/Cursor acquisition" at $60B in Q2 2026 exits. — [gravity.fast](https://gravity.fast/blog/ai-agent-funding-tracker-q3-2026/); [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-exits-ipo-ma-soar-ai-q2-h1-2026/)

### Inferences
- Firms converge on three bets:
  1. Outcome- or labor-priced vertical agents and AI-native services, including roll-ups.
  2. Coding agents.
  3. The "picks and shovels" for agents: security, governance, observability, evals and payments.
- Investors say they avoid horizontal workflow tools, consumer assistants and thin wrappers, because frontier labs keep absorbing those features (see the shutdowns in section 5).
- Bessemer's supernova/shooting-star split is the clearest articulation of the "ARR quality" worry inside a bullish firm.

### Gaps
- Primary 2026 theses from Lightspeed, Greylock, Insight, Kleiner Perkins, Index and Conviction (Sarah Guo) were not retrieved in this pass.
- Sequoia's "AI's $600B question" follow-ups and any explicit "agentic economy" piece were not verified. The Sequoia AI Ascent 2026 keynote ("This is AGI") was seen only as a podcast listing.
- No Bessemer 2026 State of AI edition was found. The 2025 report (Aug 2025) is the latest retrieved.

## 5. Skeptic signals: agent washing, pilot failure, ARR quality, margins, bubble talk, shutdowns

### Takeaway
There are several consistent warnings. Gartner expects more than 40% of agentic projects to be canceled by 2027, and says only about 130 of "thousands" of agentic vendors are real. MIT NANDA found 95% of GenAI pilots deliver no P&L impact. Menlo finds only 16% of enterprise "agents" are truly agentic. Margin and ARR-quality scrutiny is rising. The 2026 shutdowns are mostly horizontal tools absorbed by labs. None of this has yet dented aggregate funding.

### Cited Findings
- **Gartner** (press release 2025-06-25) predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027 because of "escalating costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls." It coined "agent washing," the rebranding of assistants, RPA and chatbots as agents, and estimates only about 130 of thousands of agentic AI vendors are real. The prediction draws partly on a January 2025 poll of 3,412 webinar attendees. — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027); still cited in July 2026: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/robertszczerba/2026/07/07/why-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-may-be-canceled-by-2027/)
- Gartner separately projects 40% of enterprise apps will feature task-specific agents by end-2026, up from under 5% in 2025. — cited via [Menlo Ventures](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/) search summary
- **MIT Project NANDA**, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" (July/Aug 2025), found that 95% of GenAI pilots delivered no measurable P&L impact despite $30–40B of enterprise spend. Only 5% of custom enterprise tools reach production. The method was 52 interviews, 153 leader surveys and 300 public deployments. Critics note a narrow ROI definition (about 6 months), a small sample, and no peer review. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/18/generative_ai_zero_return_95_percent); [Virtualization Review](https://virtualizationreview.com/articles/2025/08/19/mit-report-finds-most-ai-business-investments-fail-reveals-genai-divide.aspx)
- **Menlo**: only 16% of enterprise deployments are "true agents." — [Menlo Ventures](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)
- **Bessemer**: "Supernova" AI companies often have "fragile retention and thin margins." — [Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-state-of-ai-2025)
- **Gross margins and ARR quality** (commentary, not primary data):
  - AI-first startups are said to run 50–60% gross margins vs 80–90% for SaaS. — [Foundra](https://www.foundra.ai/key-reads/ai-founder-gross-margin-problem-50-percent-killing-2026-seed-rounds)
  - Only 11–14% of enterprise pilots reportedly reach scale, and valuations are said to rest on pilot counts. — [XYZBytes](https://www.xyzbytes.com/blog/ai-agent-startup-cash-crunch); [BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/ai-series-a-crunch-investor-requirements-2026)
  - A Work-Bench survey of 30 investors found 68% want $1–1.5M ARR for a standard Series A, and 85% want $2–3M for a strong one. — [BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/ai-series-a-crunch-investor-requirements-2026) (secondary)
  - These are blog sources with unclear methods, so treat them as sentiment signals rather than data.
- **"SaaSpocalypse"**: software stocks fell 22% relative to the S&P 500, described as worse than the dot-com bust. That pressures late-stage SaaS companies that raised at 25–40x ARR while public comparables trade near 5x. — [BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/ai-series-a-crunch-investor-requirements-2026); [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/saas-stock-crash-ai-agents-2-trillion-2026/) (secondary; not verified against market data)
- **2026 shutdowns** (TechCrunch "AI graveyard," 2026-09-15):

### Inferences
- The skeptic evidence is mostly about enterprise adoption and ROI (Gartner, MIT, Menlo) and unit economics (margins, retention). It has not shown up in funding totals, which hit records in H1 2026.
